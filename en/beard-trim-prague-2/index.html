A beard with shape

Our advice

How to choose a beard shape

It pays to choose a beard shape according to your face shape. For a rounder face, a slightly longer chin and sharper lines usually work, as they visually lengthen the face. For a square face, more rounded shapes soften the jaw. For a longer face, a shorter chin and fuller sides are usually better.

During the consultation, the barber will tell you what suits your face and the density of your beard. If you have inspiration, bring a photo.

Razor and sensitive skin: finishing the lines with a razor or shaver gives the sharpest result but can irritate the skin. If you have sensitive skin, ingrown hairs or a tendency to redness, tell the barber – they will adapt.

How long should you grow your beard before the first trim? A few weeks is usually enough to have something to shape. The barber can tell if it is worth waiting a little longer.

Aftercare

Beard care at home

  • Wash your beard regularly with a gentle product and avoid harsh shampoos that dry out the skin.
  • Use beard oil or balm. It softens the hairs and keeps the skin beneath them in good condition.
  • Comb or brush your beard – the hairs line up and the beard looks neater.
  • After a razor shave, avoid alcohol-based products for a few hours so the skin is not irritated.
  • Book a trim usually every two to three weeks so the beard keeps its shape.

FAQ

FAQ: Beard trim

How much does a beard trim cost in Prague 2?

A beard trim costs 420 CZK, a standalone trimmer 200 CZK. A beard trim is also part of the VIP Cut (990 CZK) and VIP All Inclusive (1,190 CZK). Everything is in the price list.

What is included in a beard trim?

The trim includes work with clippers and trimmer, finishing of the lines with a shaver or razor and a finishing balm or cologne.

What is the difference between a beard trim and Trimmer only?

A beard trim is a full service with shaping and finished lines. Trimmer only is a quick touch-up with the trimmer for 200 CZK.

Can I have my hair and beard done at the same time?

Yes. The VIP Cut and VIP All Inclusive include both a haircut and a beard trim in one visit.

What should I prepare before my visit?

Come with your beard at the length it is. Do not shorten it at home just before your visit, so the barber has something to shape. If you have inspiration, bring a photo.

Is a beard trim suitable for sensitive skin?

If you have sensitive skin or ingrown hairs, tell us in advance. The barber will adapt the procedure and recommend aftercare.

How often should I get a beard trim?

Usually once every two to three weeks, depending on growth and the shape you want.
