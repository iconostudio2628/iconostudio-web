Barber

A barber in central Prague 2

ICONO STUDIO is a modern barbershop on Bělehradská in Vinohrady. A men's haircut, a haircut combined with a beard trim or a beard trim on its own – choose the service according to what you need right now. Every cut comes with styling and a finishing balm or cologne.

The barbershop is part of one studio together with the nail studio, so both halves of the brand meet under one roof. You will find the nail and beauty services on the Nail studio page.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which barber services do you offer?

Men's haircut, Beard trim, Men's skincare & hair wash.

How do I book?

Message us on WhatsApp or send an SMS to +420 773 867 999, or just give us a call. The message “I'd like to book an appointment” fills in automatically.

What are your opening hours?

Mon–Fri 9:00–19:30, Sat 10:00–19:00. We are closed on Sundays, or open by reservation – message us and we will arrange it.

Where can I find the full price list?

All services and prices are on the price list page. Prices are in Czech crowns (CZK).
