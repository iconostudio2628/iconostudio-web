About the service

Nails sculpted to your taste

Our advice

Gel, acrylic or Gel X: which should you choose?

Gel is a flexible material that is cured in a lamp. Gel nails feel light and natural, so they suit everyday wear and anyone trying artificial nails for the first time.

Acrylic is made by mixing a liquid with a powder and hardens in the air. It is strong and suits longer or bolder shapes. A typical smell is noticeable during application.

Gel X uses pre-shaped gel tips. It is a modern alternative for anyone who wants an extension with a light result.

Nail shape: the most common are square, round, oval, almond and coffin. Square and round are practical, oval and almond visually lengthen the fingers and coffin is bold and fashionable. We will choose the shape together, or send us some inspiration on WhatsApp.

If you want extra-long nails, expect a surcharge – the price list shows it as 50, 150 and 200 CZK. Not sure? Tell us how you use your hands and how long you want your nails to be, and we will recommend the material and the shape.

Process

How sculpting works

  1. 01

    Consultation

    We agree the material (gel, acrylic or Gel X), the length, shape and colour. We are happy to see an inspiration photo.

  2. 02

    Nail preparation

    The natural nail is prepared so that the sculpting holds well.

  3. 03

    Sculpting

    The nail is shaped to the required length and form and cured.

  4. 04

    Colour and nail art

    The nails are coloured and, if you wish, decorated – glitter, rhinestones, ombré, French tips or hand-painting.

  5. 05

    Finishing

    The surface is smoothed and the nails are treated so the result looks clean and neat.

Aftercare

Aftercare and refills

  • A refill is usually needed after two to four weeks, depending on how fast your nails grow. A timely refill preserves the shape and reduces the risk of breakage.
  • Do not pull off a lifted piece – you risk damaging the natural nail. Book a repair instead. If a single nail breaks or lifts between refills, the single nail repair service costs 70 CZK.
  • Use cuticle oil regularly and wear gloves when cleaning.
  • Do not take artificial nails off yourself. Removal of artificial nails costs 250 CZK.
  • If you want to change the colour, the price list shows a colour change on artificial nails (under 10 days) at 350 CZK. We will confirm the exact conditions when you book.

FAQ

FAQ: Gel & acrylic nails

How much do gel nails cost in Prague 2?

New gel or acrylic nails with colour cost 650 CZK, a refill 590 CZK and Gel X nails 650 CZK. The value package with hand spa is 700 CZK and its refill 650 CZK. Nail art, extra-long nails and other services are charged separately – everything is in the price list.

How often do artificial nails need refilling?

Usually every two to four weeks, depending on how fast your nails grow and how you use them. When you book a refill, you choose the cheaper refill option.

What is the difference between gel and acrylic?

Gel is more flexible and is cured in a lamp, acrylic is stronger and hardens in the air. Both give your nail length and shape; they differ mainly in how they feel to wear and how well they suit different shapes. We are happy to recommend one based on your lifestyle.

What is Gel X?

Gel X is an extension technique using pre-shaped gel tips. It suits a light, natural-looking result.

How much does removal of artificial nails cost?

Removal of artificial nails costs 250 CZK, removal of Shellac gel polish alone 200 CZK and of Gellak 150 CZK. Please have it done at the studio and do not take the nails off yourself.
