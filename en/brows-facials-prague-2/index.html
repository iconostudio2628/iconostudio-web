Our advice

Shaping, or tinting with shaping?

If your brows are fuller and dark enough, shaping alone is often enough. If your brows are light, uneven or sparse, tinting with shaping is worth it. The difference in price is 100 CZK.

Brows are usually shaped every three to five weeks, depending on how fast the hairs grow. Before an important event, plan your appointment a few days in advance so that your brows have time to settle into a natural look.

Tinting – what to expect: the colour evens out the shade of your brows, accentuates their shape and visually thickens them. It works well if your brows “disappear” on your face or you do not want to use a pencil or powder every morning. We choose the shade according to your hair colour and your wishes.

Facial care – who it is for: anyone who wants to give their skin more than the usual wash and cream. Facial massage also brings pleasant relaxation. Tell us what your skin needs and we will adapt the procedure.

Brows go very well with eyelash extensions – together they give your eyes a clear frame. And if you want to treat yourself to more, take a look at Head Spa.

Aftercare

Aftercare after shaping and tinting

  • After shaping, avoid make-up around the brows for the first few hours so the skin is not irritated.
  • After tinting, do not use an intensive peeling straight away and avoid saunas so the colour lasts longer.
  • If you have an allergy or sensitive skin, tell us before tinting.
  • For facial care, please come without make-up so your skin can be cleansed properly.
  • Between visits, please do not pluck your brows yourself. That way the shape we created together stays clean and we can maintain it at your next visit.

FAQ

FAQ: Brows & facials

How much does brow shaping cost in Prague 2?

Brow shaping alone costs 100 CZK, tinting combined with shaping 200 CZK. Facial care with massage costs 750 CZK. You will find everything in the price list.

How long does brow tint last?

Usually several weeks, depending on your skin type and care. The colour fades gradually, which is why tinting is repeated together with shaping.

What does facial care include?

It is a cosmetic facial treatment combined with massage. We adapt the procedure to your skin type and are happy to explain it when you book.

How often should I have my brows shaped?

Usually once every three to five weeks, depending on how fast your hairs grow back.

Who is brow tinting suitable for?

For anyone with light, uneven or sparse brows who wants a more defined shape without daily make-up. If you are allergic to dyes, please tell us in advance.

Can I come in wearing make-up?

For brow shaping, yes; for facial care and massage, please come without make-up. The skin cleanses better that way and the treatment is more effective.
