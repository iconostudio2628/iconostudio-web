About the studio

One address, two crafts

You'll find ICONO STUDIO at Bělehradská 77 in Prague 2, in Vinohrady, close to I. P. Pavlova and Náměstí Míru. Under one roof we combine a nail studio and a barbershop: men's haircuts and beard trims, manicure, gel and acrylic nails, pedicure, eyelash extensions, brows and Head Spa.

Every service has its own page, and prices are in the clear price list. Book a time online or call +420 721 376 737. We are open Mon–Fri 9:00–19:30, Sat 10:00–19:00; on Sundays we are closed, or open by reservation.
