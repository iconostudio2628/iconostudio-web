About the service

A moment for your skin – and for you

A facial is not only for special occasions. Every day your skin has to cope with make-up, city air, air conditioning and too little sleep. At ICONO STUDIO, Bělehradská 77 in Prague 2, near I. P. Pavlova, we offer facial care and massage – a treatment that lets both your skin and you relax.

We adapt the treatment to your skin type and what it needs right now. When you book, tell us whether your skin is dry, sensitive or rather oily, and we will talk it through on site. You will find the price below and in the price list.

Our advice

Who a facial is for

When your skin looks tired. After a demanding week, travelling or in winter, when heating dries the skin out, a facial is a pleasant reset. Facial massage also helps release tension we often do not even notice in our face.

When you want to relax. The treatment is also a moment of calm: you lie down, switch off and someone else takes care of your skin. It is a treat for yourself or a gift for someone close to you.

Before an important event. Planning a wedding, a photo shoot or another occasion? Book a few days in advance so your skin has time to settle.

Sensitive skin or allergies? Tell us in advance. With a skin condition, inflammation or irritation, please consult a doctor first – a cosmetic treatment does not replace medical care.

You can combine a facial with brow shaping or with Head Spa. For men, our barbershop also offers men's skincare.

Aftercare

How to prepare and what to do afterwards

  • Please come without make-up if you can – the skin cleanses better and the treatment is more effective.
  • The day before, avoid intensive peeling and new products you have not tried yet.
  • After the treatment, give your skin a rest: ideally skip make-up for the rest of the day and avoid the sauna.
  • Protect your skin from the sun. A cream with SPF is worth it in every season.
  • Drink enough water and moisturise your skin regularly with a cream that suits it.

FAQ

FAQ: Facials

How much does a facial cost in Prague 2?

Facial care and massage costs 750 CZK. Brow shaping, which you can add, costs 100 CZK, tinting with shaping 200 CZK. You will find everything in the price list.

What does facial care include?

It is a cosmetic facial treatment combined with massage. We adapt the procedure to your skin type and are happy to explain it when you book.

Can I come in wearing make-up?

Preferably not. Without make-up the skin cleanses better and the treatment is more effective.

Is a facial suitable for sensitive skin?

We adapt the procedure to your skin type, but please tell us in advance about sensitive skin or allergies. With a skin condition or inflammation, consult a doctor first.

Are facials for men too?

Yes, anyone can book facial care and massage. Men can also choose men's skincare in our barbershop.

How often should I have a facial?

It depends on your skin and what you expect from the treatment. We are happy to advise you during your visit.

How do I book?

You can book a time online, or just give us a call on +420 721 376 737.
