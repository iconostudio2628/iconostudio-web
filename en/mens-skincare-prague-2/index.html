About the service

Care that goes beyond hair

A barbershop is not just about haircuts. At ICONO STUDIO, Bělehradská 77 in Prague 2, you can also book men's skincare, the VIP face wash, a head massage or a hair wash – on their own or as an add-on to your haircut.

The services are designed to leave you feeling fresh and well groomed after your visit. Prices are below and in the price list.

Our advice

When each service is right

Men's skincare / VIP face wash is for you if you want to give your skin deeper care. The steam and mask relax the skin, and hydration helps it not feel tight after shaving.

Head massage works as an add-on to a haircut or on its own when you need to release tension. If you want more, there is also the more detailed Head Spa.

Hair wash is appreciated by anyone who wants clean hair before styling. The Classic Cut, Premium, VIP and VIP All Inclusive include a hair wash in the price.

You can combine the care with a visit to the barber – when you book, tell us what you want to combine and we will arrange a suitable time.

If you need a haircut or beard trim along with the care, take a look at men's haircuts and beard trims.

Aftercare

Caring for your skin between visits

  • Wash your face with a gentle men's product twice a day and moisturise with a cream.
  • After shaving, use an alcohol-free balm or cream so the skin is not irritated.
  • If your skin is sensitive or problematic, tell us when you book.
  • How much care your skin needs and how often a skincare treatment is worth it, we are happy to advise during your visit.

FAQ

FAQ: Men's skincare & hair wash

How much does men's skincare cost in Prague 2?

Men's skincare / VIP face wash costs 850 CZK. A head massage costs 150 CZK and a hair wash starts from 100 CZK. You will find everything in the price list.

Who is men's skincare for?

Any man who wants to give his skin care beyond the usual wash – especially after shaving or with dry and stressed skin.

What does men's skincare include?

Face wash and massage, skin cleansing, a hydrating treatment (steam and mask) and a moisturising cream.

Is a hair wash included in the price of a haircut?

Yes, for the Classic Cut, Premium Cut, VIP Cut and VIP All Inclusive a hair wash is included. For the student cut and the children's haircut it is not listed.

Can I book just a head massage?

Yes. A head massage costs 150 CZK and you can book it on its own.
