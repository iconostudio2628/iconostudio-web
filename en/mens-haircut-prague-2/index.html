About the service

A barbershop in central Prague 2

ICONO STUDIO is a barbershop and nail studio in one, at Bělehradská 77 in Prague 2, close to I. P. Pavlova and Náměstí Míru. We offer men's haircuts as a complete service: not only the cut itself, but also styling and a finishing balm or cologne. Depending on the option, we add a hair wash, massage or a beard trim.

The barber cuts come in several levels, so you can choose exactly what you need. There are also discounted options for students and children. All prices are below and in the price list.

Our advice

Which cut should you choose?

Want simply a good haircut? Choose the Classic Cut. Want some relaxation with it? Choose the Premium Cut, which also includes a massage. If you need your beard trimmed too, take the VIP Cut, and if you want to enjoy everything, there is VIP All Inclusive.

Do you come regularly? Coming for a haircut every two weeks takes 100 CZK off all cuts. We are happy to explain the details of the discount on site. A regular haircut is the easiest way to always look well groomed.

How to prepare: come with an idea or a photo of a haircut you like. The barber will tell you what suits your hair and head shape, and advise you on styling at home.

Process

How a barber visit works

  1. 01

    Consultation

    You tell us what you want or show a photo. The barber advises what suits you.

  2. 02

    Hair wash

    For the cuts that include it (Classic, Premium, VIP, VIP All Inclusive).

  3. 03

    Haircut

    A tailored cut with scissors and clippers to the agreed shape.

  4. 04

    Beard and massage

    A beard trim with the VIP Cut, a massage with Premium and VIP All Inclusive.

  5. 05

    Styling and finish

    Styling and balm or cologne – this is how you leave looking sharp.

Aftercare

How to keep your cut at home

  • Wash your hair with a shampoo that suits you and use a styling product regularly – your barber will tell you which one.
  • Book regular haircuts, usually every three to four weeks depending on hair length. Shorter cuts with fades are worth refreshing a little more often.
  • If you have a beard, add a beard trim to your cut – the VIP Cut combines both.
  • If you come every two weeks, take advantage of the 100 CZK discount on all cuts.

FAQ

FAQ: Men's haircut

How much does a men's haircut cost in Prague 2?

The Classic Cut costs 640 CZK, the Premium Cut 770 CZK, the VIP Cut 990 CZK and VIP All Inclusive 1,190 CZK. The student cut up to age 18 starts at 540 CZK and a haircut for children up to 8 costs 400 CZK. Current prices are in the price list.

What is the difference between the Classic and Premium Cut?

Both include a hair wash, haircut, styling and balm or cologne. The Premium Cut also includes a massage.

Is a beard trim included in the VIP Cut?

Yes. The VIP Cut includes a hair wash, haircut, beard, styling and balm or cologne. VIP All Inclusive adds a massage.

Is there a discounted price for students?

Yes, the student cut up to age 18 starts at 540 CZK and includes a haircut, styling and balm or cologne.

Is there a discount for regular customers?

If you come for a haircut every two weeks, there is a 100 CZK discount on all cuts. We are happy to explain the details.
