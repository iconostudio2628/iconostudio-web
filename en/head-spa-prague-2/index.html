About the service

A moment just for your head

Head Spa is care that combines relaxation with a nourishing routine for your scalp and hair. A slow pace, pleasant sound, massage and water – you leave relaxed. You can treat yourself at ICONO STUDIO, Bělehradská 77 in Prague 2.

The service costs 890 CZK and includes five steps. Below you will find what they involve and what they are for. The full price list is on the price list page.

What you get

Five steps for one price

From calming sound through massage to a regenerating oil. Everything is included in the Head Spa price.

  1. 01

    White noise therapy

    A steady, calming sound that dampens background noise and helps you relax. It forms a quiet sound backdrop to the whole treatment.

  2. 02

    Treatment of acupuncture points on the head

    Targeted pressure on selected points on the head, used to release tension.

  3. 03

    Scalp exfoliation

    A gentle peeling that helps cleanse the scalp of dirt and styling product residue.

  4. 04

    Asian-style hair washing and head massage

    Slow, rhythmic washing combined with massage. It is the main relaxing part of the treatment.

  5. 05

    Blow-dry and regenerating oil

    Your hair is blow-dried (without styling) and treated with a regenerating hair oil. You leave with smooth, tidy hair.

Our advice

Who Head Spa is for

Head Spa is for anyone who wants to relax, look after their scalp and hair or enjoy a moment without rushing. It suits the end of a demanding week, a gift for someone close to you, or a regular self-care ritual.

It is not a medical treatment. If you have scalp problems, such as eczema or psoriasis, or your scalp is sensitive and irritated, tell us in advance and consult a doctor as well.

How often? According to your taste and needs. Some people treat themselves to Head Spa as a regular ritual, others on special occasions or as a gift for someone close. We are happy to give you a recommendation during your visit.

Blow-drying is part of the care, but without styling. If you want your hair styled for a particular occasion, take a look at hair wash and head massage or men's haircuts.

Aftercare

What to keep in mind before and after your visit

  • You do not need to do anything special before your visit. Come relaxed and in comfortable clothes.
  • After your visit, do not weigh your hair down with heavy styling products, so the regenerating oil can do its work.
  • If you have a sensitive scalp or an allergy to cosmetic products, let us know in advance.
  • Allow plenty of time. Head Spa is slow care that is best enjoyed without rushing – we will tell you the length of the visit when you book.
  • You can combine Head Spa with other services, for example manicure or pedicure, and make your visit a complete moment for yourself.

FAQ

FAQ: Head Spa

How much does Head Spa cost in Prague 2?

Head Spa costs 890 CZK. It includes white noise therapy, treatment of acupuncture points on the head, scalp exfoliation, Asian-style hair washing and head massage, and blow-drying with regenerating oil.

Does Head Spa include hair styling?

Your hair is blow-dried, but not styled. If you want your hair styled for a particular occasion, arrange it with us when you book.

Is Head Spa suitable for scalp problems?

It is a relaxing and caring service, not a treatment. For eczema, psoriasis or irritation, please consult a doctor first and let us know when you book.

How often should I repeat Head Spa?

According to your taste and needs – some people have it regularly, others on special occasions. We are happy to give you a recommendation during your visit.

Is Head Spa suitable for all hair types?

The care is intended for the scalp and hair in general. If your hair is coloured or damaged, or you have a sensitive scalp, tell us – we are happy to advise.

What is white noise therapy?

It is a steady, calming sound backdrop that dampens background noise and helps you relax.
