FAQ

Offer: frequently asked questions

How big is the discount?

The discount is 15%. It applies at ICONO STUDIO from Monday 9 to Sunday 15 November 2026 to every service except the barber.

When does the offer end?

On Sunday 15 November 2026. From Monday 16 November the regular price list applies.

Which services does the discount cover?

All nail and beauty services in our price list: manicure, pedicure, gel and acrylic nails, nail art, eyelash extensions, brows, facials and Head Spa. The barber (men's haircuts, beard trims, men's skincare and hair washing) is not part of the offer.

How do I book for the offer?

You can book online or call +420 721 376 737. Every service page on the site links straight to its own booking.

How much will I pay after the discount?

The price of each service is listed in the price list above. The 15% discount is taken from those prices during the offer. If you are not sure, call us on +420 721 376 737.

Are you open at the weekend during the offer?

During the week we are open Mon–Fri 9:00–19:30, and on Saturday 14 November 10:00–19:00. On Sunday 15 November we are closed, or open by reservation.

Where is ICONO STUDIO?

At Bělehradská 643/77, 120 00 Prague 2 – Nové Město, by the I. P. Pavlova metro station and around Náměstí Míru. More on the Contact page.
